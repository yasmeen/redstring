    How Red String works

    1. Screen — Scout checks each lead against your signed clients' audiences and neighborhoods, and says pitch, pitch with a distinct angle, or decline.
    2. Pitch with spec work — Researcher, Auditor and Producer build spec posts for the business, every claim tied to a source.
    3. Double Take — Before anything ships, it's compared with every other client reaching the same people. Lookalikes are blocked and revised.
