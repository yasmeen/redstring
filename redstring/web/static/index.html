        What Red String is

        An agency's AI team that never makes lookalike videos for clients who share an audience

        Agents screen leads, research, and draft social videos for local businesses. Before anything posts, Red String checks it against every other client that reaches the same people, and you approve what goes out.

        The problem

        Two clients, the same parents, the same trick

        When the gym copies the art studio's signature move, parents see both videos and neither business stands out.

          How it's built: every lead, audience, technique and decision is a node in FalkorDB, and agents reach it only through named queries.

          1 · Architecture

          Client work stays private. Only technique lists are shared.

          Each prospect or client keeps its own graph. Only a Fingerprint, the technique IDs a draft uses, is projected into the shared agency graph. Cross-client checks run there; copy, posts and goals never leave the client.

          P1Q0Q1Q2

          Stays in the client's graph: posts, scripts, goals Crosses to the shared graph: technique IDs only Shared: who reaches whom, the technique list

          Private work stays separate while shared fingerprints support cross-client checks.

          2 · Screening

          Every lead is scored against every signed client

          Scout follows each lead's audiences, and the audiences they overlap, to every signed client. The path weights add up to a conflict score per client, and the highest one sets the verdict. Pick a lead to trace it.

          Q1lead_conflict

          0max 0
          Board call 0.50Distinct angle 0.90
          Reaches this audience strongly Audiences that overlap Pick a lead row to trace it

          Know a lead's client overlap before deciding to pitch.

          3 · Double Take

          Lookalike drafts are blocked before they reach the same people

          A draft is blocked when it shares rare techniques with a business that reaches the same people. Risk is similarity times audience overlap; anything at the threshold or above is caught before it ships.

          Q2double_take

          similarity×audience overlap=risk ≥ 0.35 → blocked and revised

          Blocked work goes back to the Producer before it can reach the same people.

          4 · Creative territory

          Rare techniques already in use by businesses on the same audience

          A signature is a rare technique in 30% or more of a business's work. Red marks the territory behind this run's blocked drafts: the same rare techniques, used by businesses that reach the same people.

          Q0derive_signatures

          Small, medium, large: share of the business's work Behind a blocked draft Hover a dot for the exact share

          A signature move is how people recognize a business; don't hand it to a neighbor.
